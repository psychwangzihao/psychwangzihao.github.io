Welcome to the **Consciousness Lab at Zhejiang University**! Our group explores the cognitive and neural mechanisms underlying conscious experience, using approaches from cognitive neuroscience.

Our research themes include:

- **Consciousness** — self-model, absence of consciousness
- **Mental imagery** and **aphantasia**
- **Cognitive neuroscience** of perception and awareness
