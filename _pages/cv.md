  <div class="cv-fail-item">
    <h4>My first study — a single case that could not be finished<span class="cv-fail-when">2026</span></h4>
    <p>
      My first independent project was a single-case investigation of an acquired silent reading
      comprehension disorder: a person who could no longer understand text when reading silently,
      but could the moment they read aloud or followed an external beat. I built the whole
      apparatus — twelve computerised tasks spanning three writing systems and three input
      modalities, three standardised questionnaires, and a structured interview — and collected
      and analysed the data myself.
    </p>
    <p>
      It could not be completed as designed. A single case cannot be replaced by recruiting
      someone else, because there is only one such person; the project now stands as a single-case
      report rather than the comparison it was meant to support.
    </p>
    <p>
      What it left behind is method, not results: analyse the raw data through scripts rather than
      through any summary table; fix the exclusion criteria before seeing the data, and make them
      independent of the hypothesis; and treat any significant effect as provisional until it
      survives dropping one participant at a time.
    </p>
  </div>
