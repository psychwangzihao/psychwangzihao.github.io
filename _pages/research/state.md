<p class="r-p">
  Content is what appears; state is the condition a brain is in while anything appears at all.
  Anesthesia, sleep, wakefulness and the many gradations in between are the obvious cases, but
  the same variation exists within a single resting scan. This project treats that variation as
  measurable rather than as noise.
</p>
