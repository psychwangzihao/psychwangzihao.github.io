<p style="font-size: .95rem; line-height: 1.7; color: var(--global-text-color); max-width: 46rem;">
  <em>As it is, not as it ought to be.</em> The question of what consciousness ought to be —
  whether it is fundamental, what it is for, what would count as an explanation — has a long
  philosophical literature and I am not adding to it. The question I can work on is narrower and
  answerable: <strong>what is a person actually conscious of, and what kind of brain is having
  that experience?</strong>
</p>

<p style="font-size: .95rem; line-height: 1.7; color: var(--global-text-color); max-width: 46rem;">
  Those two halves come apart cleanly, so the section does too. <strong>Content</strong> is what
  can be reported — imagery, inner speech — and it is measured behaviourally and with causal
  intervention. <strong>State</strong> is the level and mode a brain is in while any content
  appears at all, and it is modelled from resting-state imaging.
</p>
