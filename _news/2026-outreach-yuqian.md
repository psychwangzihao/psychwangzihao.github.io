Gave a psychology science-outreach talk at <strong>Yuqian High School</strong> (Lin'an, Hangzhou), bringing mind and brain science to high-school students. Built an interactive demo for the talk, <strong><a href="/demos/perception/">感觉在哪里？ / Where Do Senses Live</a></strong> — thirteen hands-on scenes, opening on an illusion that makes the whole hall doubt their own eyes, and closing on a question I can't answer: does a tree feel pain?
